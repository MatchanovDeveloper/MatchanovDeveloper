## 👋 About Me

I'm a **data analyst growing into data engineering**. I take raw business data and turn it into clean, decision-ready dashboards. I extract and transform data with **SQL/PostgreSQL** and **Python (pandas)**, design **star-schema data models**, write **DAX measures**, and build interactive **Power BI** reports. I also build sales and revenue dashboards in **Excel** using Power Pivot and SUMIFS.

On the engineering side, I practice **ETL** by generating synthetic CRM data and designing the keys and joins between its tables.

> 🎯 **Currently looking for:** a Data Analyst or Junior Data Engineer role where I can build reliable data pipelines and reports and turn numbers into clear recommendations.
